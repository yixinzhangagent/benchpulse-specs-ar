## 1. The One Spatial Moment & Why It Belongs on Glasses

When you are soldering a microcontroller breakout board at an electronics workbench, your right hand holds a **350°C soldering iron** and your left hand feeds **0.6 mm solder wire**. Looking away at a laptop schematic or tapping a phone screen with flux-covered fingers breaks your eye-hand alignment on sub-millimeter pads and risks bridging pins or overheating components.

**BenchPulse** turns Snap SPECS into a hands-free spatial electronics bench coach that combines all three core strengths of AR glasses:
1. **Shares your view:** Locks onto the workbench PCB in your downward gaze (`12°` below eye level, `z = -85 cm`) and tracks which joint you are soldering.
2. **Draws in the world:** Places a glowing `3.6 cm` spatial target ring directly over the active PCB pad (`C1 +3.3V`, `U1-P8 GND`, `J2-P1 I2C_SDA`, `J2-P2 I2C_SCL`) and anchors thebench multimeter station `35°` to your right (`x = +48 cm`).
3. **Leaves your body free:** Hands stay on the soldering iron and wire; when a joint is finished, a single forgiving pinch on a large `9 cm × 4.5 cm` target (`NextStepButton`) collapses the completed joint into a quiet green check dot and moves the spatial ring to the next pad.

## 2. Four AR Design Habits Applied

| AR Design Habit | How BenchPulse Implements It in Lens Studio |
|---|---|
| **1. Put information on the thing it's about (Not a floating website)** | Pin polarity (`+3.3V`, `GND`, `I2C_SDA`, `I2C_SCL`) and the `3.5s` thermal ring sit `1.5 cm` directly above the corresponding 3D pad on `BenchPcbRoot` (`z = -85 cm, y = -20.5 cm`). |
| **2. Show it when needed, then get out of the way** | Unvisited pads stay hidden or show as quiet dots; completed pads shrink to `0.35×` green dots. The continuity short alert (`FaultPopupRoot`) and edge arrow (`WayfinderArrow`) are completely hidden until a short occurs (`Rule G3`: disabled at the tail of `frame.onInitialized`). |
| **3. A few big targets — Pinch is the new tap** | Every SIK/UIKit `Button` is `8.5 cm × 4.5 cm` to `15.0 cm × 4.5 cm` at `85–90 cm` distance (exceeding Snap's `4–6 cm` minimum target guideline) and placed at low-arm desk height (`y = -8 to -12 cm`) to prevent arm fatigue. |
| **4. Keep it inside the narrow `37° × 32°` FOV & design for additive display** | Main task elements fit inside a `34 cm × 24 cm` envelope at `z = -85 to -90 cm` (well within SPECS' `41 cm × 59 cm` usable FOV at `85 cm`). Because black renders as transparent on SPECS' additive optics, all typography uses high-luminance cyan (`#4DF0FF`), amber (`#FFB830`), emerald (`#38F29B`), and white (`#F4FAFF`) via `applyTextRole()`. |

## 3. Honest Preview Stand-Ins (`logic proven` vs. `feel unproven`)

Because Lens Studio's Interactive Preview runs on a laptop without physical SPECS glasses, live SnapML PCB fiducial recognition, or a physical Bluetooth multimeter, `devpost/design-brief.md` defines three explicit **Stand-In buttons** on `BenchPulseDiagnosticsUI`:
- **`Found Board` (`LockBoardButton`):** Stand-in for camera fiducial lock. Snaps the 3D spatial overlay onto the PCB and activates Step 1 (`C1 +3.3V`).
- **`Heat Pad 3.5s` (`HeatPadButton`):** Stand-in for soldering iron tip contact. Starts the 3.5-second thermal timer ring on the active pad and transitions `TimerStatusText` to `"Heating: 350°C — Flow Ready"`.
- **`Simulate Short` (`TriggerShortButton`):** Stand-in for a Bluetooth multimeter continuity bridge (`0.2 Ω`). Plays `ContinuityAlert.wav`, reveals the in-view `WayfinderArrow` pointing right toward `MultimeterStationRoot` (`x = +48 cm`), and opens the fault recovery card (`AcknowledgeFaultButton`).

## 5. How to Run & Verify in Lens Studio 5.22+

1. Open `BenchPulse.esproj` in **Lens Studio 5.22 or later** (Target Platform: `Spectacles`).
2. In the **Preview Panel**, select **Spectacles (Stereo)** in **Interactive Preview** mode (`WASD` to walk, `Q/E` for height, right-drag to look down `12°` at the workbench, left-click to pinch).
3. Open the **LEAF Panel** (or run `/specs-leaf-run-in-preview` in Claude Code / Cursor with CLAD installed) to execute all 5 scenarios registered in `Assets/Tests/LeafIndex.ts`.
